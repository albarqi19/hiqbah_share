SECTION 1 — PURPOSE OF THIS FILE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

This file defines the Sales Management System — the sub-module that governs
the front-facing sales operations layer: how sales representatives and sales
staff capture orders, manage their customer portfolios, track commissions,
monitor payment collection, handle leads, and monitor their own performance
through role-scoped dashboards.

This file is distinct from and complementary to File 06 (Advanced Roastery
Order Workflow), which defines the backend order lifecycle — the multi-stage
routing from sales review through accounting, payment, production, packaging,
shipping, and closure. This file defines the sales-facing experience on top
of that lifecycle: what the rep sees, what the sales manager sees, how
commissions are calculated and disbursed, how leads progress to customers,
and how the sales team is organized around customers and territories.

Together, these two files define the complete sales operation:

  FILE 06: Order Workflow
    The backend pipeline. Stages, approvals, payment gates, allocation,
    production trigger, shipping, closure. The contractual logic.

  FILE 16 (this file): Sales Management
    The sales layer. Rep experience, customer portfolio, lead pipeline,
    commission engine, payment tracking, sales KPI dashboard, and
    Sales Intelligence. The human-facing operational layer.

The source file identifies one critical operational failure that this
module must correct:

  CRITICAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE (elevated from source):
  Commissions must be fully automatic. The moment a customer payment
  is confirmed in the Accounting Module, the commission status transitions
  automatically from Pending to Approved to Paid — without manual
  calculation, manual entry, or reliance on anyone remembering to
  update a spreadsheet. Manual commission calculation is where most
  small B2B businesses lose rep trust and make expensive errors.

SECTION 2 — CLEAN TECHNICAL SUMMARY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Sales Management System is a tenant-scoped, role-partitioned sales
operations layer that:

  1. Provides a structured ORDER CREATION interface for sales reps (mobile-
     first) and sales staff (web), with customer selection, product line
     entry, price resolution from the Pricing Module, rep assignment, and
     commission rate capture — feeding directly into the order workflow
     defined in File 06.

  2. Provides a CUSTOMER PORTFOLIO system where each customer is assigned
     to one or more sales reps with defined commission splits. A rep sees
     only their own assigned customers (server-side scope enforcement).

  3. Provides a LEAD PIPELINE for capturing and progressing prospective
     customers from first contact through conversion to active customer.

  4. Provides a fully AUTOMATED COMMISSION ENGINE that computes commission
     amounts from order value and rate at order creation, and advances
     commission status automatically when payment is confirmed in the
     Accounting Module.

  5. Provides PAYMENT TRACKING visibility for sales reps and sales managers
     over outstanding B2B invoices — so the rep knows which customers to
     follow up with and what is blocking their own commission from being paid.

  6. Provides role-scoped SALES DASHBOARDS: a rep sees their own
     performance, their own customers, their own commissions. A Sales
     Manager sees all reps, all customers in their scope, and team-level
     KPIs. Neither role sees data outside their scope through any surface.

  7. Provides EVENT LINKAGE: upcoming trade events, product launches, and
     marketing campaigns are surfaced in the sales context so reps can
     connect leads and follow-ups to operational triggers.

  8. Provides the foundation for SALES INTELLIGENCE (Phase 2): predictive
     signals identifying customers likely to place an order soon, customers
     at risk of churning, and cities or territories with highest growth
     potential.

The entire module is read-heavy. Its primary write operations are:
order creation, lead creation and progression, customer assignment changes,
and commission rate configuration. All financial transactions (invoices,
receipts, payments) remain in the Accounting Module.

SECTION 3 — ORIGINAL BUSINESS INTENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The author describes a business that relies heavily on field sales
representatives to develop the B2B wholesale channel. The reps are the
primary channel for acquiring new cafés and restaurants as customers, placing
and following up on orders, and maintaining relationship quality in the field.

Without a structured digital tool, the author identifies three common
operational failures:

  FAILURE 1: Commission disputes.
    Reps do not trust manual commission calculations. They do not know
    when an order qualifies for commission, how much they are owed, or
    when it will be paid. This breeds distrust and reduces motivation.

  FAILURE 2: Invisible payment collection risk.
    B2B sales frequently involve deferred payment terms (Net 15, Net 30).
    Without visibility, overdue invoices accumulate silently. By the time
    the finance team notices, the rep has moved on and the relationship
    is strained. Early visibility enables early follow-up.

  FAILURE 3: Lost leads and forgotten follow-ups.
    A rep meets a promising café owner at a trade show. Without a lead
    capture tool, the contact information goes into a WhatsApp message
    that is never actioned. Leads die in the noise.

The module is designed to eliminate all three failures through automation
(commissions), visibility (payment tracking), and structure (lead pipeline).

The author also raises a forward-looking aspiration: Sales Intelligence —
a system that tells reps which customers are most likely to order soon
(based on their order frequency patterns), which are at risk of stopping
(based on declining frequency), and which cities have the highest untapped
potential. This is designated Phase 2 in this rewrite.

4.2  ORDER CREATION
────────────────────

Order creation in this module is the entry point that feeds File 06's
order workflow. It is NOT a separate order system — it is the sales
team's interface to the order workflow.

The interface differs by surface:
  - MOBILE (rep): optimized for quick capture in the field (at a
    café, on a call). Minimal taps, prefilled defaults, offline support.
  - WEB (sales staff, admin): full form with all fields visible, useful
    when creating bulk or complex orders at a desk.

Order creation steps:

  STEP 1 — CUSTOMER SELECTION
    The user selects from their assigned customer pool.
    For a rep: only customers assigned to them are shown.
    For a sales staff / admin: all customers in their branch scope.
    If the customer does not exist, an inline customer creation is
    triggered. The new customer requires: Company Name, Contact Person,
    Mobile, City, Business Type (Café / Restaurant / Store / Distributor /
    Other), and rep assignment.

    Critical backend rule: The server validates at order submission that
    the selected customer is within the requesting user's assignment scope.
    Client-side filtering is a UX convenience only.

  STEP 2 — PRODUCT LINE ENTRY
    The user adds order lines. Each line:
      - Product selection from the active product catalog
        (SKUs that are visible to this channel and tenant).
      - Quantity.
      - Price: resolved automatically by PriceResolutionService
        (per File 10 — hierarchy: customer-specific → volume tier →
        wholesale list → base price). The rep cannot change the price
        directly. If a custom price is needed, it must be:
        a. Configured as a customer-specific price in the Pricing Module
           (with approval), OR
        b. Flagged as a Special Price Request during order creation,
           which puts the order in a "Pending Price Review" sub-state
           before it enters the standard Sales Review stage.

    The rep never sees the cost or margin of a product. They see only
    the applicable selling price for their customer. Margin data is
    restricted to Finance / Management roles.

  STEP 3 — REP AND COMMISSION ASSIGNMENT
    The rep creating the order is automatically assigned as the primary
    rep (created_by_rep_id is set from the session). If commission split
    applies (shared client with two reps), the system shows the split
    configuration from the ClientAssignment record and the commission is
    computed accordingly. The rep does not set the commission rate —
    it is pulled from the rep's configured rate or the client-specific
    override.

  STEP 4 — ORDER NOTES AND PRIORITY
    Optional notes field. Priority: Normal / Rush / Standing.
    Rush orders require rep to provide a reason (free text, mandatory).

  STEP 5 — SUBMISSION
    Order submitted via POST /sales-orders (per File 06 API).
    System generates order number (tenant-scoped sequence).
    Status → New Rep Order.
    CommissionRecord created with status = PENDING at this point,
    linked to the order and the rep(s).
    Sales team notified.

  Offline buffering:
    On mobile, if the rep submits while offline, the order is buffered
    locally with an idempotency key and submitted when connectivity
    is restored. The rep sees a "Pending sync" indicator.
    The system rejects duplicate submissions of the same idempotency key.


4.3  CUSTOMER PORTFOLIO (MY CLIENTS)
──────────────────────────────────────

Every customer is owned by one primary rep, with optional secondary reps
defined in the ClientAssignment record (with percentage split for commission).

Customer lifecycle states (sales perspective):
  - PROSPECT (Lead not yet converted)
  - ACTIVE (placed an order within the last N days; configurable per tenant)
  - INACTIVE (no order within N days; alert generated)
  - AT_RISK (declining order frequency detected; Phase 2 signal)
  - CHURNED (no order within M days; configurable; Phase 2 classification)

Customer record fields visible to a rep:
  - Company Name, Contact Person, Mobile, City, Business Type
  - Date of First Order, Date of Last Order
  - Total Sales to Date (from their orders; read from SalesOrders)
  - Active Order Count (open orders)
  - Outstanding Balance (from AR Aging via Accounting Module)
  - Days Since Last Order
  - Assigned Rep(s) with commission split
  - Customer Status (Active / Inactive)
  - Notes log (free text entries by the rep; timestamped)

What a rep CANNOT see:
  - Other reps' customers.
  - Cost / margin data.
  - Internal accounting details beyond their own commission.
  - Internal production or QC data.

What a Sales Manager CAN see:
  - All customers in their branch / territory scope.
  - All reps' portfolios.
  - Full sales data across all reps.
  - AR aging per customer (linked to Accounting Module view).

Server-side scope enforcement:
  Every API call to customer data includes a scope filter derived
  from the session:
    - Rep: WHERE primary_rep_id = session.user_id
            OR (EXISTS in ClientAssignment with user_id = session.user_id)
    - Sales Manager: WHERE branch_id = session.branch_id
    - Admin / CEO: no branch restriction (tenant_id filter only)
  This filter is applied at the query layer, not the presentation layer.


4.4  LEAD PIPELINE (LEADS MANAGEMENT)
────────────────────────────────────────

A Lead is a prospective customer who has not yet placed an order.
Leads are created by reps in the field and by sales staff on web.

Lead record:
  - id, tenant_id, branch_id
  - place_name, city, contact_person, mobile
  - business_type (same enum as Customer)
  - interest_level (LOW | MEDIUM | HIGH)
  - source (FIELD_VISIT | TRADE_EVENT | REFERRAL | INBOUND | OTHER)
  - linked_event_id (nullable; links to an Event from File 12 if the
    lead was sourced from a trade show or product launch event)
  - notes (free text; rep notes from the interaction)
  - status (NEW | CONTACTED | SAMPLE_SENT | QUOTATION_SENT | CONVERTED
            | LOST)
  - created_by (rep user_id), created_at
  - assigned_to (rep user_id; may differ from creator if reassigned)
  - last_activity_at, next_followup_date
  - converted_customer_id (populated when status = CONVERTED)
  - loss_reason (populated when status = LOST; free text)

Lead status lifecycle:
  NEW → CONTACTED (rep made first contact)
      → SAMPLE_SENT (product sample shared with the prospect)
      → QUOTATION_SENT (a price quotation was shared)
      → CONVERTED (lead accepted and became a Customer; order created)
      → LOST (prospect declined; reason recorded)

Rules:
  - A Lead can only be CONVERTED when a SalesOrder is created for the
    converted customer and the order is in Sales Review or later stage.
    The conversion links lead.converted_customer_id and marks the customer
    with lead_origin = this lead's id for future attribution tracking.
  - A rep can only see and manage leads assigned to them or created by them.
    Sales Manager sees all leads in their scope.
  - Leads with next_followup_date passed and no activity in 7 days
    generate an OVERDUE_FOLLOWUP alert to the rep.
  - If a lead has been in status NEW for more than 14 days without
    any status change, a STALE_LEAD alert is generated.

Event linkage:
  When a trade event ("Coffee Expo Riyadh") is recorded in the Events
  Module (File 12), reps can create leads directly from within that event
  context. The lead.linked_event_id is set, and the Events Module tracks
  how many leads and eventual customers originated from each event — a
  direct ROI signal for event attendance decisions.


4.5  CLIENT ASSIGNMENT AND COMMISSION SPLIT
────────────────────────────────────────────

ClientAssignment entity manages the relationship between a customer and
the reps responsible for them:

  ClientAssignment:
    - id, tenant_id, customer_id
    - assignments[] — each entry:
        { rep_user_id, commission_share_pct, is_primary, effective_from,
          effective_to (nullable), assigned_by, assigned_at }
    - total_commission_share_pct must always equal 100%
    - version (for history tracking — every change creates a new version)

Rules:
  - At any point in time, the sum of active commission_share_pct across
    all active assignment rows for one customer must equal exactly 100%.
  - Changing a commission split after orders are in progress does NOT
    retroactively affect commissions on existing open orders. The split
    at the time of order creation is the governing split. New orders
    use the current (updated) split. This is enforced by capturing the
    commission_split_snapshot on the CommissionRecord at order creation.
  - Transferring a customer from one rep to another creates a new
    ClientAssignment version. The old version is closed (effective_to = today).
    Orders created under the old version continue with the old commission rules.
  - Management can share a customer between up to N reps (configurable per
    tenant; default max = 3 reps per customer).
  - Self-assignment by a rep is not permitted; only Sales Manager or Admin
    can assign customers to reps.

Commission rate source hierarchy (evaluated at order creation):
  1. Customer-specific commission rate (CommissionOverride per customer)
  2. Product category commission rate (if configured)
  3. Rep's default commission rate (from SalesAgentProfile)
  4. Tenant default commission rate (from SalesSettings)

The commission rate is captured in CommissionRecord at the moment of
order creation and is immutable thereafter. Any rate changes apply
only to future orders.


4.6  COMMISSION ENGINE (FULLY AUTOMATED)
─────────────────────────────────────────

The commission engine eliminates manual calculation entirely. It operates
as a pipeline triggered by order and payment events.

Commission lifecycle:

  STEP 1 — ORDER CREATED
    On SalesOrder creation, CommissionRecord(s) are created:
      - One record per rep (for split commissions, one per rep)
      - commission_amount = order_grand_total × commission_rate_pct / 100
        (or per-line computation if rates differ by product category)
      - status = PENDING
      - order_id, customer_id, rep_user_id, commission_rate, commission_amount
        are captured and become immutable
      - commission_split_snapshot captured from current ClientAssignment

    Important: Commission is computed on order_grand_total EXCLUDING VAT.
    A commission of 5% on a 5,000 SAR + 750 VAT order = 5% × 5,000 = 250 SAR.
    Never computed on the VAT-inclusive total. This must be explicit in
    the engine and documented in the rep agreement.

  STEP 2 — ORDER CONFIRMED (Payment Confirmed)
    On Payment Confirmed event (per File 06):
    CommissionRecord status → APPROVED
    The rep receives a notification: "Commission approved for Order #XXX"

    If PARTIAL PAYMENT:
    CommissionRecord status remains PENDING until the payment threshold
    is met (configurable per tenant: 100% of invoice, or a configurable
    minimum payment % such as 50%).

  STEP 3 — COMMISSION PAYMENT SCHEDULED
    At the end of each commission payment cycle (weekly / monthly /
    per tenant policy), the accounting team initiates the Commission
    Payout batch. This generates a PayrollCommissionPayment record per
    rep for all APPROVED commissions in the cycle.

    The Finance user reviews the batch and approves it.
    On approval:
    CommissionRecord status → PAID
    PayrollCommissionPayment is posted to the Accounting Module as
    a salary/commission expense (Account 6100 or sub-account per tenant
    CoA, per File 02).
    The rep receives a notification: "Commission paid for N orders."

  STEP 4 — ORDER CANCELLED OR RETURNED (Reversal)
    If an order is cancelled after commission was APPROVED or PAID:
    A CommissionReversal record is created.
    APPROVED commissions are reset to CANCELLED.
    PAID commissions trigger a deduction in the next commission cycle
    (negative line in the next payout batch).
    The rep is notified of the reversal and the reason.

  Commission status states:
    PENDING → APPROVED → PAID
                       → CANCELLED (from APPROVED; if order cancelled)
    PAID → REVERSED (deduction scheduled in next payout)

  Rules enforced server-side:
    - Commission amount is never manually edited after creation.
      Changes to commission (e.g., a dispute) require a CommissionAdjustment
      record with reason and approver. The adjustment creates a new
      credit/debit entry; it does not modify the original record.
    - A rep cannot approve their own commission. Finance / Accounting
      role holds commission.approve permission.
    - Commission payout batch requires Finance Manager approval.
    - Commission records are tenant-scoped and rep-scoped; a rep sees
      only their own commissions.


4.7  PAYMENT TRACKING
──────────────────────

Payment Tracking gives the sales team visibility into outstanding B2B
invoices so they can proactively follow up before relationships deteriorate.

The Payment Tracking view is a READ from the Accounting Module's AR Aging
data. This module does not own any payment records — those belong to
Accounting (File 02). It surfaces a sales-relevant view of them.

Payment Tracking displays per rep (scoped to their customers):

  Outstanding Invoice List:
    - Customer Name, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Due Date
    - Invoice Amount, Amount Received, Outstanding Balance
    - Days Overdue (positive = overdue; negative = not yet due)
    - Payment Status (UNPAID | PARTIALLY_PAID)

  Aggregated view:
    - Total Outstanding per customer
    - Total Overdue across all customers
    - Customers exceeding credit limit (from Accounting credit policy
      per File 02)

Sales rep follow-up actions from the Payment Tracking screen:
  - Add a follow-up note (logged to the customer record and shared
    with the Accounting team).
  - Request an extended payment term (creates a Payment Term Request
    linked to the invoice; routed to Finance for approval).
  - Mark customer as "Contacted about payment" with timestamp.
    This creates a PaymentFollowUpLog entry.

What the sales rep CANNOT do from this screen:
  - Record a payment receipt (that is Accounting's function).
  - Modify an invoice.
  - Override a credit limit.

Commission connection:
  The payment tracking view also shows the rep which of their invoices
  are unpaid and therefore blocking their commission from advancing
  from PENDING to APPROVED. This creates a direct incentive for reps
  to actively support collection — their own commissions depend on it.

  REP_SALES_DECLINE               | Manager    | Rep's monthly sales dropped
                                  |            | > configurable % vs. prior
  HIGH_VALUE_CUSTOMER_INACTIVE    | Manager    | A VIP customer (top 20% by

                                  |            | configured credit limit

All alert thresholds are tenant-configurable in SalesSettings.
Notification delivery channels: iOS push, Android push, in-app, email.


4.9  EVENT LINKAGE
───────────────────

The sales module integrates with the Events Module (File 12) to create
a connection between operational events and sales activities.

When a trade event, product launch, or marketing campaign is created
in the Events Module:
  - It becomes visible in the sales team's event feed.
  - Leads captured at the event can be tagged with linked_event_id.
  - After the event, the system shows a "Post-Event Follow-Up" task list
    for all leads captured at that event.
  - Sales performance associated with the event (orders from converted
    event leads) feeds into the event's ROI calculation.

This connection transforms events from passive calendar entries into
active sales pipeline triggers — a capability the source file specifically
calls out as a differentiator.

When a new coffee product is launched (ProductLaunchEvent from File 12):
  - The system identifies customers who regularly purchase that origin,
    process, or product category.
  - A SAMPLE_REQUEST_SUGGESTED notification is sent to the reps
    responsible for those customers.
  - This creates an immediate follow-up opportunity without requiring
    the rep to manually review the product catalog.


4.10  SALES AGENT PROFILE
──────────────────────────

SalesAgentProfile:
  - user_id (links to User from File 01)
  - employee_id, full_name, city, hire_date
  - default_commission_rate_pct
  - commission_payment_cycle (WEEKLY | BI_WEEKLY | MONTHLY)
  - performance_metrics (computed; not stored):
      customer_count, active_customer_count, total_sales_ytd,
      avg_monthly_sales, total_orders_ytd, commission_earned_ytd,
      commission_pending, commission_paid_ytd, conversion_rate
  - assignment_history[] (previous customer assignments; for audit)
  - employment_status (ACTIVE | ON_LEAVE | TERMINATED)

When a rep is TERMINATED:
  - All their customer assignments are placed in UNASSIGNED status.
  - The Sales Manager receives an alert listing all affected customers.
  - Commissions in PENDING or APPROVED status at the time of termination
    are handled per the termination policy (configurable; default: APPROVED
    commissions are paid in the final cycle; PENDING commissions are
    held until the linked orders are paid, up to a maximum holdback period).
  - A terminated rep's data is never deleted — it is retained for
    audit, historical reporting, and commission dispute resolution.

4.12  SALES INTELLIGENCE (Phase 2)
────────────────────────────────────

Rule-based predictive signals to support proactive sales behavior.
No decisions are automated — these are signals for reps to act on.

  LIKELY_TO_ORDER_SOON:
    Customers whose historical order pattern suggests an order is due.
    Computation: if the customer's median reorder interval is N days,
    and their last order was ≥ (N × 0.8) days ago, they are flagged
    as "likely to order within the next few days."
    Surface: priority band in the rep's customer list.

  CHURN_RISK:
    Customers whose order frequency has declined by > 30% vs. their
    own 90-day baseline for two consecutive periods.
    Surface: red flag on the customer record; alert to rep and manager.

  TERRITORY_GROWTH_OPPORTUNITY:
    Cities where customer density (existing customers per registered
    café / restaurant) is low relative to the tenant's successful
    markets. Identifies geographic whitespace for rep prioritization.
    Surface: territory map / city ranking in Sales Manager dashboard.

  REP_COACHING_SIGNALS:
    Identifies specific performance patterns per rep that differ
    from the team average: e.g., high lead count but low conversion
    rate → coaching focus on closing; high AOV but low customer count
    → coaching focus on prospecting.
    Surface: individual rep performance summary for manager review.

SECTION 6 — WORKFLOW / PROCESS LOGIC
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

6.1  WORKFLOW A — REP CAPTURES AN ORDER (MOBILE)

  1. Rep opens mobile app → sees their dashboard.
  2. Taps "New Order" → selects customer from their assigned portfolio.
  3. Adds products and quantities. Prices auto-filled from PriceResolutionService.
  4. Reviews total. If a special price is needed, flags as Special Price Request.
  5. Submits order offline or online (idempotency key generated client-side).
  6. Server creates: SalesOrder + CommissionRecord(s) in a single transaction.
  7. Notification sent to Sales team for review.
  8. Rep sees order in their "Open Orders" list with status "New Rep Order."

6.2  WORKFLOW B — LEAD CAPTURED AT A TRADE EVENT

  1. Rep is at the Coffee Expo Riyadh trade event (linked_event_id set).
  2. Rep opens mobile app → Lead section → New Lead.
  3. Enters place name, city, contact, business type, interest level.
  4. Sets next_followup_date.
  5. Lead created with status NEW and linked_event_id.
  6. After the event, rep receives a "Post-Event Follow-Up" task list.
  7. Rep contacts the lead → updates status to CONTACTED.
  8. Sends a sample → SAMPLE_SENT.
  9. Shares a quotation → QUOTATION_SENT.
  10. Lead places first order → CONVERTED. Customer record created.
      converted_customer_id set on lead. Lead is closed.

6.3  WORKFLOW C — COMMISSION AUTO-ADVANCEMENT

  1. Order #452 created. CommissionRecord created: PENDING. Amount = 250 SAR.
  2. Order progresses through review, production, packaging, shipping.
  3. Customer makes full payment.
  4. PaymentReceived event fired by Accounting Module.
  5. CommissionEngine receives event: checks all CommissionRecords
     linked to orders of this customer with PENDING status.
  6. Confirms payment threshold met.
  7. CommissionRecord status → APPROVED.
  8. Rep receives push notification: "Commission approved — Order #452 — 250 SAR"
  9. At month-end, Finance generates Commission Payout batch.
  10. All APPROVED commissions for the period included.
  11. Finance reviews and approves the batch.
  12. CommissionRecord status → PAID.
  13. Payroll expense posted to Accounting Module.
  14. Rep receives push notification: "Commission paid — 3 orders — 730 SAR total."

6.4  WORKFLOW D — CUSTOMER REASSIGNMENT

  1. Sales Manager opens the Client Assignment screen.
  2. Selects customer "Café XYZ" currently assigned 100% to Rep A.
  3. Transfers to Rep B (100%) effective today.
  4. System:
     a. Closes the current ClientAssignment entry (effective_to = today).
     b. Creates a new ClientAssignment entry for Rep B (effective_from = today).
     c. All CommissionRecords from orders CREATED BEFORE the transfer
        retain their original assignment (Rep A's commission).
     d. All new orders from today forward are attributed to Rep B.
  5. Rep A is notified: "Café XYZ has been transferred from your portfolio."
  6. Rep B is notified: "Café XYZ has been added to your portfolio."

SECTION 9 — ACCOUNTING / COSTING IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The Sales Management System has one significant accounting impact:
commission payouts.

When a CommissionPayoutBatch is approved and paid:
  - The Accounting Module records: Dr Commission Expense / Cr Bank/Cash
    (or Cr Salaries Payable if included in payroll).
  - The expense is posted against account 6100 (Salaries & Wages) or
    a dedicated Commission sub-account per tenant CoA (File 02).
  - The payment is linked to the employee's payroll record (HR Module).

All other accounting impacts (invoicing, receipts, AR) belong to
the Accounting Module (File 02). The Sales Management System reads
them but does not write them.

Commission computed on VAT-exclusive order value:
  This is a binding accounting rule. Commission = order_net_amount ×
  rate (not order_total_with_VAT × rate). This prevents the rep from
  being overcompensated by VAT amounts that pass through the business.
  The commission_base_amount field stores the VAT-exclusive base used
  for the calculation. This is documented here for the Accounting
  Module's expense reconciliation.

11.3  SERVER-SIDE VALIDATIONS

  - Customer scope filter applied from session on every customer query.
  - Commission amount computed server-side; never trusted from client.
  - CommissionRecord immutability: commission_amount, commission_rate_pct,
    commission_base_amount cannot be updated after creation.
    Corrections are CommissionAdjustment records only.
  - ClientAssignment split sum must equal 100% at all times.
  - Rep cannot self-assign customers.
  - Commission approval: rep_user_id ≠ approving user.
  - Payout batch includes only APPROVED commissions from the period.
  - Lead conversion: a SalesOrder for the converted customer must
    exist in Sales Review or later status.
  - Commission on VAT-exclusive total: the base is always
    SalesOrder.subtotal (before VAT), never SalesOrder.grand_total.

11.4  DATABASE TRANSACTIONS

  Order creation + CommissionRecord(s) = single ACID transaction.
  Payment confirmed event → CommissionRecord APPROVED + audit log
    = single transaction.
  ClientAssignment transfer: close old version + create new version
    + audit log = single transaction.
  CommissionPayoutBatch approval + CommissionRecord status PAID updates
    + Accounting expense event + audit log = single transaction.
  Lead conversion: create Customer + close Lead + first SalesOrder link
    + audit log = single transaction.

11.5  FAILURE SCENARIOS

  - PaymentConfirmed event received but CommissionEngine fails to
    advance the record: event stored in retry queue; re-processed
    with exponential backoff; Commission stays PENDING until processed;
    Finance sees "Commission Advancement Delayed" alert.
  - CommissionPayoutBatch accounting posting fails: batch reverts to
    APPROVED state; commission records remain APPROVED (not PAID);
    Finance alerted; retry available.
  - Lead linked_event_id references a deleted event: the lead record
    is preserved; event linkage shows "Event deleted" gracefully.
  - Rep device offline; order submitted with idempotency key; reconnects
    and retries: second submission rejected with idempotent response
    (returns the already-created order).

SECTION 12 — SAAS READINESS REVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Multi-tenancy:
    All records (Lead, ClientAssignment, CommissionRecord, etc.) carry
    tenant_id. Every query filters by tenant_id from session.
    A rep in Tenant A cannot see Tenant B's customers or commissions
    under any circumstances.

  Per-tenant configuration (SalesSettings):
    Customer inactive threshold, churn threshold, lead stale threshold,
    commission payout cycle, commission payment trigger (100% or partial%),
    max reps per customer, commission on VAT-exclusive (default true),
    commission rate defaults, notification thresholds, sales intelligence
    toggle (Phase 2).

  Subscription gating:
    - Phase 1: Full operational module (order creation, customer portfolio,
      leads, commissions, payment tracking, dashboards, notifications).
    - Phase 2: Sales Intelligence (churn risk, likely-to-order, territory
      growth, rep coaching signals).

  SaaS risks:
    - Commission data is financially sensitive. Cross-tenant leakage
      would expose one business's rep compensation to another.
      Mitigation: tenant_id filter on every CommissionRecord query;
      rep scope filter within tenant; tested invariants.
    - A rep in one tenant using a stolen session tries to access another
      tenant's customer list. Mitigation: tenant_id from session, not
      from request body; session verification per File 01.

    - Android: push notification

SECTION 15 — DASHBOARD / REPORTING IMPACT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Sales reports are defined in the Reports Center (File 15, Domain C —
Reports C-01 through C-08). The dashboards defined here are operational
(real-time, role-scoped) rather than period-defined report snapshots.

  REP DASHBOARD (real-time, own scope):
    My Customers: total, active, inactive, new this month
    My Orders: open, completed, in collection
    My Sales: total this month, vs. last month, AOV
    My Commissions: pending, approved, paid this month
    My Leads: open pipeline by stage, overdue follow-ups

  SALES MANAGER KPI DASHBOARD (real-time, team scope):
    Team Revenue: total, by rep (ranked), growth %
    Pipeline: total leads, by stage, conversion rate
    Customer Health: active, inactive, at-risk count
    Collections: outstanding, overdue, over credit limit
    Commissions: total earned, total pending payout

  COUNTING RULES:
    - Commission figures in dashboards and reports always exclude
      CANCELLED and REVERSED commissions from totals.
    - "Active Customer" count uses the tenant-configured threshold
      (default 30 days). If the threshold is changed, the count
      updates immediately on the next dashboard refresh.
    - A customer with an open order but no confirmed shipment counts
      as Active.
    - Rep sales figures include only orders in status Shipped,
      Delivered, or Closed — NOT orders in earlier stages. This
      prevents reps from inflating their "sales" by creating orders
      that are never fulfilled.

SECTION 16 — DEVELOPER IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  1. The CommissionEngine must be designed as an event-driven service,
     not a polling job. It subscribes to domain events (OrderCreated,
     PaymentConfirmed, OrderCancelled) via the internal EventBus. Using
     a polling job risks delays and missed events. If events are
     dispatched via an outbox pattern (per Accounting Module File 02
     design principle), the Commission Engine is a consumer of that
     outbox.

  2. CommissionRecord.commission_amount is computed at order creation
     and stored permanently. Do NOT recompute dynamically from the
     current order total — orders may be edited (through the revision
     workflow in File 06) and the commission must reflect the terms
     agreed at order time. If an order revision changes the total,
     a CommissionAdjustment is created for the delta; the original
     record is not modified.

  3. The ClientAssignment split sum (100%) must be enforced at the
     database level with a check constraint on the sum of active
     commission_share_pct per customer, in addition to application-level
     validation. A rep share update that brings the total above or
     below 100% must be rejected even if it passes application validation.

  4. "Repeat Last Order" on mobile: implementation approach — store
     the last N orders per customer × rep combination in a
     RepOrderHistory cache. The mobile app retrieves this to prefill
     the order form. The prefilled data is advisory; the server validates
     the full order on submission regardless.

  5. Lead conversion must be atomic: the Lead.status → CONVERTED, the
     Customer record creation (if new), and the first SalesOrder linkage
     must all succeed or all roll back. A partial conversion (lead marked
     converted but customer not created) creates an orphaned lead state
     that corrupts pipeline reporting.

  6. Commission payout batch generation should be idempotent: if a batch
     is generated twice for the same period, the second generation must
     detect the existing batch and return it rather than creating a
     duplicate. Key: (tenant_id, period_start, period_end, rep_user_id).

  7. For the Payment Tracking view (§4.7), the query must join
     SalesOrders → Invoices → Receipts → AR balance, filtered by
     the rep's assigned customer IDs. This query involves four tables
     and must be pre-computed or carefully indexed. On large tenants
     with many B2B customers, this can be a performance hotspot.
     Recommend: nightly pre-aggregation with intraday cache invalidation
     on payment events.

SECTION 17 — EDGE CASES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  EC-01: A customer is transferred from Rep A to Rep B while Rep A has 5
         open orders. Commission on those 5 orders belongs entirely to Rep A
         (captured at order creation time). Rep B receives commission only
         on new orders created after the transfer date. This is not optional —
         it is contractually required and must be provable from the
         commission_split_snapshot on each CommissionRecord.

  EC-02: Rep A and Rep B both share a customer (70%/30% split). Rep A
         creates an order. Both CommissionRecords are created simultaneously
         in the same transaction: Rep A at 70% of the commission, Rep B
         at 30%. If the transaction fails midway (e.g., Rep B's record
         fails to create), the entire transaction rolls back. Never create
         a partial commission split — either all records are created or none.

  EC-03: Rep is terminated while they have APPROVED commissions (unpaid).
         Per the configured termination policy, the APPROVED commissions
         must be included in the final payout batch. The payout batch
         generation must include commissions for TERMINATED reps; the
         batch approval flow must handle this case explicitly (Finance sees
         "includes terminated rep commissions" annotation).

  EC-04: A partial payment is received that meets the commission trigger
         threshold (e.g., 80% paid, trigger is 50%). CommissionRecord
         advances to APPROVED. Subsequently the remaining 20% payment
         is received — no additional commission event fires (the record
         is already APPROVED).

  EC-05: An order is returned after the commission was already PAID.
         A CommissionReversal is created. The reversal amount is deducted
         from the rep's NEXT commission payout batch as a negative line.
         The rep is notified. If the rep has already been terminated and
         there is no future payout, a separate deduction process is required
         per HR policy (out of scope for this system; flag for HR).

  EC-06: A customer exists in two different reps' claims simultaneously
         (data entry error — both reps "registered" the same café).
         Detection: duplicate detection on (customer mobile OR company name +
         city) during customer creation. The system warns of a potential
         duplicate and shows the existing record. Admin must resolve the
         conflict before both records persist.

  EC-07: A lead is captured at a trade event but the event is later
         cancelled in the Events Module. The lead.linked_event_id remains
         set; the event record is soft-deleted. The lead's event reference
         shows "Event Cancelled" in the UI. The lead itself is unaffected —
         it continues through its lifecycle normally.

  EC-08: A Sales Manager who is also a rep (in a small team, this is
         common) needs to approve their own commission. The system blocks
         self-approval regardless of role. A second Finance Manager or the
         Tenant Admin must approve. If no second approver exists, the
         Tenant Owner must approve. This edge case must be handled in the
         payout batch UI — "1 commission requires approval by a different
         user" annotation.

  EC-09: Commission rate changes mid-cycle. New rate effective from today.
         Orders created today and after: new rate. Orders created before
         today: old rate (from commission_split_snapshot on CommissionRecord).
         When the Finance team generates the payout batch, they may see two
         different rates for the same rep in the same period — this is
         correct and must be clearly displayed.

  EC-10: Rep submits the same order twice (network timeout, tapped twice).
         Idempotency key prevents duplicate creation. Second submission
         returns the already-created order ID with HTTP 200 (not an error).
         Rep's UI updates normally. No duplicate CommissionRecords created.

SECTION 18 — QUESTIONS / AMBIGUITIES / RISKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

OPEN QUESTIONS:

  Q1: Is commission computed on the full order value, or only on specific
      product categories (e.g., commission on coffee only, not on equipment)?
      WHY IT MATTERS: The commission engine design changes significantly
      if different rates apply to different product categories within the
      same order. The source implies a flat order-level rate. Confirm.
      Recommendation: support both models; per-category rate is a higher-
      complexity option gated behind a configuration flag.

  Q2: What is the commission payment trigger threshold — 100% payment,
      or does partial payment (e.g., 50%+) qualify?
      WHY IT MATTERS: Setting it at 100% means reps only earn on fully
      settled invoices, incentivizing collection follow-up. Setting it
      lower reduces collection motivation but is more common in practice.
      Recommendation: 100% default; tenant-configurable.

  Q3: Is there a minimum order value threshold below which no commission
      is earned?
      WHY IT MATTERS: A SAR 50 order generates a SAR 2.50 commission at 5%.
      Processing overhead may exceed the commission value. Recommendation:
      configurable minimum commission threshold (default: none).

  Q4: What happens to commissions on orders that are partially returned
      (some products returned, others kept)?
      WHY IT MATTERS: The commission should be reduced by the returned
      portion. The system needs to know the returned line amount to compute
      the reversal correctly. This requires the return/Credit Note workflow
      to report per-line values, not just a total amount.

  Q5: Are external sales agents (independent contractors, not employees)
      in scope, or are all reps internal employees?
      WHY IT MATTERS: External contractors have different tax treatment
      (no social insurance, different withholding rules in Saudi Arabia).
      If external reps are in scope, the commission payout batch must
      distinguish between employee payroll and contractor payments.
      Recommendation: Phase 1 = internal employees only; Phase 2 = support
      for external contractors with separate payment flow.

  Q6: Can a rep see their own commission rate, or is it confidential?
      WHY IT MATTERS: If reps can see their own rate, they can compute
      expected commissions and contest discrepancies. This is
      beneficial for trust. However, if rates differ between reps, a
      visible rate might create inter-rep friction.
      Recommendation: reps see their own rate; cannot see others' rates.
      The view is scoped to own profile only.
